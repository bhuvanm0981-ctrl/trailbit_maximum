What is Trailbit?

Trailbit helps you make your first contribution to an open-source project. It finds real issues that maintainers have marked as good for newcomers, checks that they are still alive, explains the problem, and walks you through fixing it one step at a time.

New to open source? Three words to know
Issue
A bug report or a request on a project's GitHub page.
Fork
Your own copy of a project, where you can make changes safely.
Pull request
How you offer your change back to the maintainers for review.

How it works, in four layers

You move through them one at a time. Nothing is hidden behind a login, and you can step back whenever you like.
